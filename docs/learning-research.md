# Learning department and budget research

Checked 12 October 2026. All 100 starting accounts have a record in `shared/ld-research.json`, linked by seedId. Research used public web searches and selected source-page reads, including official careers pages, annual reports, benefits policies and employer job listings. Some evidence was available only as indexed excerpts. The record preserves source links and source periods; this is not direct confirmation from the company.

28 accounts have evidence naming a learning team or function; 69 have a programme or learning benefit without enough evidence of a dedicated department; 3 remain unconfirmed. These categories describe evidence found, not proof that a department exists today or does not exist. Function-specific teams do not establish company-wide ownership.

12 accounts have a quantified published amount. Distinguish employee allowances, per-employee spending, historical company spending and partial programme spending. No current company-wide purchasing budget was established. Do not multiply an allowance by headcount, convert historic spending into current budget, or claim it is available to Maven. Retain currency, geography, eligibility, period and restrictions. Unknown is not zero.

Prioritize discovery with learning owners. Confirm current role, external-course eligibility, approval authority, available budget and buying cycle before qualifying an opportunity. This research does not verify any contact email or authorize outreach.

## Buyer shortlist — 12 October 2026

Reviewed ten accounts: GitLab, Adobe, HubSpot, Cisco, Freshworks, Tech Mahindra, Persistent Systems, Atlassian, Thoughtworks and Accenture. Selection is qualitative: internal learning evidence, relevant workforce programmes, published spending/allowances and geographic fit. It is not a predicted-conversion ranking, and no CRM customer check was possible.

Seven have sourced learning leaders, one has a provisional title, and two have conflicting employment evidence. Company biographies, an employer-hosted assessment and self-published professional evidence support roles, not legal purchasing authority. Source age and uncertainty remain visible. No direct work email was verified: production has no Hunter key and no connected verification provider. GitLab publishes a shared learning inbox; it is explicitly not the individual's address and has not passed deliverability checks. Masked addresses and email patterns were not substituted.
